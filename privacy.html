Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This policy explains what data Topic Radar ("the tool") handles. The tool is a personal, non-commercial research project run by one individual ("the operator").

1. Data we collect

  • Operator's own account: an access token for the operator's own Threads account, used only to call the Threads API.
  • Public posts: the text, timestamp, permalink and public engagement counts of public Threads posts returned by keyword searches. The tool does not request or store author usernames or profile information, and does not try to identify authors.

We do not collect private messages, follower lists, contact information, location, or any data from private accounts.

2. How we use the data

Only to count how often everyday topics are mentioned each day and to display trend charts to the operator. We do not build profiles of individuals, and we do not use the data for advertising, surveillance, eligibility decisions, or any automated decisions about people.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer the data to anyone. Individual posts are never republished. The data stays on the operator's personal computer.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored locally on the operator's password-protected computer. Access tokens are stored in a local configuration file that is not uploaded anywhere.

5. Retention

Raw post text is kept for up to 24 months for research and then deleted; aggregated daily counts (which contain no personal data) may be kept longer. If a post is deleted on Threads, we delete our copy upon request.

6. Your choices and deletion

If you believe the tool holds a copy of your public post and want it removed, see the Data Deletion page. We will delete it within 30 days.

7. Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
