Data Deletion Instructions

Topic Radar only stores public post text and timestamps returned by the Threads Keyword Search API, on the operator's personal computer.

To request deletion

  1. Open an issue at github.com/edchriswu/topic-radar-site/issues with the title "Data deletion request".
  2. Include the permalink(s) of your Threads post(s). You do not need to share any other personal information.
  3. We will delete the matching records within 30 days and confirm in the issue.

If you de-authorize the app, the operator's access token is discarded and no further data is collected.
