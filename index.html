Topic Radar

A personal, non-commercial research tool

Topic Radar is a personal research tool operated by a single individual in Taiwan. It measures how often everyday consumer and lifestyle topics (for example, product shortages, price increases, or newly popular products) are discussed in public online posts, and turns those counts into daily trend charts for the operator's own study.

It does not publish content, does not contact users, does not show ads, and is not offered to the public. Only the operator uses it, on their own computer.

How it uses the Threads API

  • The operator signs in with their own Threads account through Threads' official authorization window.
  • The tool uses the Keyword Search API to search public posts for a small set of everyday keywords a few times per day.
  • Posts are counted per day to produce trend charts. Individual posts are never republished or shared.
